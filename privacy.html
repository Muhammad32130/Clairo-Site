Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Clairo ("we", "us", "our") operates automation software for real estate brokerages. This policy explains how we handle information submitted through our lead intake forms.

Information We Collect

We collect name, phone number, message, budget, and timeline from individuals who submit inquiries through brokerage contact forms powered by Clairo.

How We Use Information

Submitted information is used solely to score and route leads to the appropriate real estate agent. We do not sell, share, or distribute this information to any third parties.

SMS Notifications

SMS notifications are sent exclusively to internal real estate agents who have opted in by texting SUBSCRIBE to +18325328125. No SMS messages are ever sent to leads, home buyers, or any external parties. Agent phone numbers are used solely to deliver lead alert notifications and are never shared with third parties.

Data Storage

Lead information is stored securely in Airtable and is accessible only to the brokerage and Clairo staff. We do not sell, rent, or share any collected data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Lead data is retained for the duration of the service agreement. Upon termination, all client data is exported and deleted within 14 days upon request.
