Our Story

BUILT IN HOUSTON.
BUILT FOR HOUSTON.

Clairo was founded in 2026 by two people who were frustrated watching local businesses struggle to keep up with automation while larger competitors pulled further ahead.

The problem wasn't effort. Local business owners work harder than anyone. The problem was access — access to the right tools, the right systems, and the right expertise.

We started with real estate brokerages because the pain was obvious. Leads coming in, agents too busy to follow up instantly, deals going to whoever called first. We built a system that fixed that in a week. The response was immediate.

That's when we knew Clairo was bigger than one industry. Every local business faces the same challenge — too much manual work, too little time, and technology that feels out of reach.

We're here to change that. One business at a time.
